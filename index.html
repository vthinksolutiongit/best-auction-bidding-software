Running kabaddi, volleyball or football auctions?

The same checklist applies to any player auction. The main extra check is point 9: confirm that the software lists your exact sport and lets you set player roles for it, such as raider and defender in kabaddi or setter and libero in volleyball.

How to test before auction day

1. Run a small test auction

Create 2 teams and 10 players on the free plan or demo. Ask two friends to bid from their phones.

2. Test on the real screen

Open the auction view on the actual LED wall or projector. Check that names and prices are readable from the back row.

3. Download the reports

Finish the test auction and download the team list. Make sure it has everything you need to print.

Frequently asked questions

What is cricket player auction bidding software?

It is an app or website that runs a live player auction for a cricket tournament. Team owners bid on players with a fixed purse, and the software records every bid, sale and team squad.

What should I check before choosing cricket auction software?

Check live bidding from phones, automatic RTM and purse rules, iPhone and Android support, reports after the auction, player registration, language, display options, other sports, support and pricing.

Is there free cricket auction software?

Several cricket auction apps offer free plans with limits on teams or players. Run a small test auction on the free plan before your real event.

Can the same software run kabaddi or volleyball auctions?

Some auction apps support several sports. Check that your exact sport is listed, not just "multi-sport" in general.

Do cricket auction apps use real money?

Most local tournament auctions use virtual points for team purses. Check each app's terms.
