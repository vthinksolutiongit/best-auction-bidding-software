## The 10-point checklist

| # | Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Live bidding from every team owner's phone | Every screen updates almost instantly, so no one argues about who bid last |
| 2 | Automatic Right to Match (RTM) | The software applies the rule with a countdown timer, not the auctioneer |
| 3 | Team purse and squad limits enforced | Teams cannot overspend or exceed squad and category limits |
| 4 | Works on both iPhone and Android | One owner who cannot log in can delay the whole auction |
| 5 | Reports right after the auction | Team-wise lists and sold/unsold players as PDF or Excel, ready to print |
| 6 | Easy player registration | Registration link or Excel upload; AI player cards remove photo backgrounds automatically |
| 7 | Your committee's language | Hindi or Gujarati support makes setup easier for local organisers |
| 8 | Display for the audience | LED screen, projector, shareable live link and YouTube overlay |
| 9 | Your sports, not just "multi-sport" | Kabaddi, volleyball, football or badminton must be listed by name |
| 10 | Clear pricing and real support | A free plan or demo, published prices and on-site help for big events |

## How to test software before auction day

1. Run a small test auction with 2 teams and 10 players on the free plan or demo.
2. Open the auction view on the actual LED wall or projector and check readability from the back row.
3. Finish the test and download the team list to make sure it has everything you need to print.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is cricket player auction bidding software?**
An app or website that runs a live player auction for a cricket tournament. Team owners bid on players with a fixed purse, and the software records every bid, sale and team squad.

**What are AI player cards?**
The organiser uploads a player's photo, and AI removes the background and creates a uniform card with the player's name, role and base price.

**Is there free cricket auction software?**
Several cricket auction apps offer free plans with limits on teams or players. Test with a small auction first.

**Can the same software run kabaddi or volleyball auctions?**
Some apps support several sports. Check that your exact sport is listed.

**Do cricket auction apps use real money?**
Most local tournament auctions use virtual points for team purses. Check each app's terms.
